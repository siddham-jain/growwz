Groww, for your first ₹100 🌱

Redesigning Groww for India's 20–26 year-old, first-time investors

Live app: {{URL_SHORT}}  ·  Full evals: {{URL_SHORT}}/evals.html

1. My take on the problem

Access is solved. Groww opens an account in minutes, and 56% of new NSE investors are now under 30. The problem is what happens after the account opens. A 22-year-old arrives with a small, irregular income, a finfluencer feed, and no idea which of 1,000+ funds is "right". The app greets them with tickers, red/green P&L and F&O one tap away.

So they either freeze (the account sits empty) or gamble: SEBI found 91% of individual F&O traders lost money in FY25, and 89% of under-30 traders lost. Both end in churn. The real job: turn an anxious first-timer into someone with a lasting habit, and get them through their first market dip. That's Groww's most valuable customer: joins at 22, stays 30 years.

Psychology I designed around: present bias → start from goals · lumpy income → flexible by default · loss aversion → calm by default · jargon shame → explain terms in place · dopamine apps → reward consistency · comparison → share %, never ₹.

2. In scope

  • Onboarding → starter plan → first ₹100 (KYC assumed done)
  • Goal-based Stashes, with the fund picked from the time horizon
  • Payday split, %-of-payment splits for gigs, skip/pause, streaks with freezes
  • Calm mode, Dip Coach and a neutral panic-sell check
  • 60-second "Bytes" lessons and tap-to-decode jargon
  • F&O reality check and cool-off, a capped "Spice pot", Squads

3. Out of scope

  • KYC/account opening: already strong on Groww
  • Live prices, payments, order routing: data is illustrative
  • Loans/BNPL: debt works against the goal
  • Crypto: unregulated in India
  • F&O trading screens and paper trading: only the gate
  • Hinglish (next step) and personalised advice (needs SEBI RIA)

4. The solution: goal-first, calm-by-default Groww

  • Ask "what's it for?", not "what to buy?" Each goal becomes a Stash. The time horizon picks the fund (Steady / Balanced / Growth) and says why. Money needed within 2 years never goes into equity; unaffordable goals become an honest first milestone.
  • Fit the money people actually have. ₹100 minimum. Salaries get a one-tap payday split; gig and pocket money get a % of each payment. Skip or pause anytime. Streaks count weeks invested, with freezes for broke months.
  • Calm by default. Home shows goal progress, not today's P&L. In a dip, Dip Coach explains what moved and what usually happens next.
  • Learn without leaving. Every jargon word is tappable, and story-style Bytes teach one idea in 60 seconds.
  • Friction only where it protects. F&O gets SEBI's loss data, a 3-question check, a one-time cool-off and a self-set loss limit; a capped Spice pot gives risk-seekers room.
  • Social, without comparison. Squads save for the same trip and share progress %, never rupees.

Why this works: it targets the two biggest churn points, the empty account and the first dip, and the first win (₹100 invested, a celebration, week 1 of a streak) lands within 12 taps.

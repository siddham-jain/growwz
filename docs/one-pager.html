1My take on the problem

Access is solved. Groww opens an account in minutes, and 56% of new NSE investors are under 30. The problem is what happens after: a 22-year-old with lumpy income, a finfluencer feed and 1,000+ funds gets greeted by tickers, red/green P&L and F&O one tap away.

So they freeze (empty account) or gamble: SEBI found 91% of F&O traders lost money in FY25. Both end in churn. The real job: turn an anxious first-timer into someone with a habit, and get them through their first dip. That's Groww's most valuable customer: joins at 22, stays 30 years.

4The solution: goal-first, calm-by-default Groww

🎯Goals, not tickers

Name it ("Goa with the gang"). The horizon picks the fund; money needed within 2 years never touches equity.

💸Fits lumpy money

Start at ₹100. Salary gets a one-tap payday split; gigs and pocket money get a % of each payment.

🧘Calm by default

Goal progress, not daily P&L. Dips come with a coach and context, never a panic button.

📚Learn in 60 sec

Story-style Bytes with one quick question. Every jargon word is tappable.

🛑Friction only where it protects

F&O: SEBI's numbers, a 3-question check, one-time cool-off, a self-set loss limit. It still unlocks.

👯Social, no flexing

Squads save for the same trip and share progress %, never rupees or targets.

Why this works: it targets the two biggest churn points, the empty account and the first dip, and the first win (₹100 invested, a celebration, week 1 of a streak) lands in 12 taps.

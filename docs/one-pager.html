Groww, for your first ₹100

Redesigning Groww for India's 20–26 year-old, first-time investors

Live app: {{URL_SHORT}}  ·  Full evals: {{URL_SHORT}}/evals.html

1. The problem

Access is solved: 56% of new NSE investors are under 30. What fails is what happens next. A first-timer with small, irregular income faces 1,000+ funds, daily P&L and one-tap F&O. They either freeze (empty account) or gamble (91% of F&O traders lost money in FY25, per SEBI). The job: build a lasting habit and get them through their first market dip.

2. In scope

  • Onboarding to first ₹100 (KYC assumed done)
  • Goal-based stashes with horizon-picked funds
  • Flexible contributions for irregular income
  • Calm mode and dip guidance
  • In-context learning and jargon decoding
  • F&O guardrails

3. Out of scope

  • KYC (already strong)
  • Live data, payments, order routing (prototype)
  • Loans/BNPL (works against the goal)
  • Crypto (unregulated)
  • Personalised advice (needs SEBI RIA)
  • Vernacular (next step)

4. Solution and why

  • Goals, not products. Users name a goal; the time horizon picks the fund. Money needed within 2 years never goes into equity. Why: removes choice paralysis and unsuitable picks.
  • Fit irregular income. ₹100 minimum, a one-tap payday split for salaries, a % of each payment for gig and pocket money, skip/pause anytime. Why: fixed SIPs bounce and feel like failure.
  • Calm by default. Goal progress instead of daily P&L; context during dips. Why: the first red number is the biggest churn point.
  • Learn in place. Every jargon term is tappable; 60-second lessons. Why: confusion makes people leave, not ask.
  • Friction only where it protects. F&O gets loss data, a short readiness check, a one-time cool-off and a loss limit. Why: guard the riskiest path without blocking it.
